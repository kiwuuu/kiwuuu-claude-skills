# Brand Voice Skill

## Trigger
USE THIS SKILL when: writing copy, social posts, website content, emails, product descriptions, ads, or any public-facing text for Kiwuuu or TraficShop. Also trigger when user says "brand voice", "on-brand", "write in our style", "keep the tone consistent".

## Kiwuuu Brand Voice

**Archetype:** The Pragmatic Futurist — a founder who ships, not a dreamer who plans.

**Tone:** Direct. Confident. Slightly provocative. No fluff. Every sentence earns its place.

**Voice characteristics:**
- Short sentences. Punchy verbs. No passive voice.
- Numbers beat adjectives ("25 agents" not "many agents")
- Specificity signals credibility ("6 posts/day" not "frequent posting")
- Talk like a founder to founders — peer level, not guru-to-student
- Humor is dry, earned, never forced
- Problems are named, not avoided
- AI is a tool, not magic — treat it that way

**What we never say:**
- "Game-changing", "revolutionary", "next-level", "cutting-edge"
- "Leverage", "synergy", "ecosystem" (without irony)
- "We're excited to announce..."
- "Our team of experts..."
- Anything that sounds like a press release

**Hook formulas that work for Kiwuuu:**
1. Confession: "I lost 4 hours last week. Here's what I learned."
2. Counter-intuition: "More agents = worse output. Here's why."
3. Concrete outcome: "25 agents. 6 posts/day. $10/month. Here's the stack."
4. Challenge: "Your automation has a memory problem. Mine does too. Fixed it."
5. System reveal: "This is the exact workflow that [result]. Thread."

**Platform adjustments:**
- **X/Twitter:** Ultra-short hooks. Thread structure. End with a question or CTA.
- **LinkedIn:** Slightly more context. Story arc. Professional but still human.
- **WhatsApp (bot messages):** Casual, helpful, no jargon. Feel like a smart colleague.
- **Website copy:** Clear hierarchy. Benefits first. Features second. CTA always present.

## TraficShop Brand Voice

**Archetype:** The Reliable Partner — established, efficient, no-nonsense B2B.

**Tone:** Professional but warm. Clear and direct. Efficiency-focused. Serbian business culture aware.

**Voice characteristics:**
- Direct value statements ("140+ products, next-day delivery")
- Trust signals woven naturally ("trusted by 200+ retailers across Serbia")
- Bilingual awareness: EN copy is international-facing, SR copy is relationship-focused
- Avoid salesy language — B2B buyers distrust hype
- Specificity on logistics and product details

**What we never say:**
- "Premium quality" (prove it, don't claim it)
- "Best prices guaranteed" (no superlatives without proof)
- "One-stop shop" (cliché)
- Anything that sounds like retail/consumer marketing

## Application Rules

1. **Read existing copy before writing new copy.** Match the register already established on the page.
2. **When in doubt, cut.** Every sentence that doesn't move the reader forward should be deleted.
3. **CTAs are specific:** "Request a Quote" not "Contact Us". "See the Catalog" not "Learn More".
4. **Never mix voices** — Kiwuuu content and TraficShop content have distinct tones. Don't blend.
5. **For social hooks:** Test the first line in isolation. If it doesn't make you want to read the next line, rewrite it.
