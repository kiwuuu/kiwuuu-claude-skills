## Win/Loss Analysis Framework

### When you WIN, ask:
1. What made them choose Kiwuuu over Respond.io/Wati?
2. What was the deciding factor?
3. What almost stopped them from buying?

### When you LOSE, ask:
1. What did they choose instead?
2. What feature/price point was the dealbreaker?
3. Would they consider Kiwuuu if [X] were different?

### Win patterns to expect for Kiwuuu:
- Price (much cheaper than Respond.io)
- Simplicity (no flow builder required)
- Specialization (legal/finance agents not in competitors)
- WhatsApp-native feel

### Loss patterns to watch for:
- Team inbox (multiple agents, Respond.io does this better)
- Integration ecosystem (HubSpot, Salesforce sync)
- Enterprise security requirements (SOC2, etc.)

## Battle Cards (Sales Objections)

### "We already use Respond.io"
```
Respond.io is great for team inboxes with multiple human agents. 
Kiwuuu is different — it's specialized AI agents that handle the 
conversation autonomously. Many businesses use both: Respond.io for 
the human handoff, Kiwuuu for the 24/7 AI-first layer.

Are you happy with the AI quality in Respond.io, or is that area 
you'd like to improve?
```

### "Wati is cheaper"
```
Wati sells you a $49 tool — then YOU build the flows, write the scripts,
and maintain it. If a customer asks anything off-script, it breaks.

Kiwuuu's Agency Tier is done-for-you: we run specialized agents for your
clients so you sell outcomes, not software seats.

Kiwuuu's agents actually understand the question and give complete 
answers, like a knowledgeable team member — not a chatbot.

Would 30 minutes to compare them side by side be worth it?
```

### "We'll just use ChatGPT"
```
ChatGPT is great for one-off tasks, but it doesn't live on WhatsApp, 
doesn't know your business, and every conversation starts from scratch.

Kiwuuu's agents are pre-configured for your industry, respond in your 
WhatsApp, and are always on — no copying/pasting required.
```

## Pricing Intelligence

### Competitor pricing signals to track
- Any move to usage-based pricing (risk: Kiwuuu needs to respond)
- Free tier expansions (increases acquisition competition)
- Enterprise plan additions (signals moving upmarket)
- Seat-based → conversation-based shifts

### Kiwuuu pricing positioning
Current: Agency Tier — $499/mo base + $19/workspace (see vault kiwuuu-pricing; legacy $49/$99 self-serve retired)
Advantage over conversation-based: no surprise bills for the agency or its clients
Advantage over seat-based: agencies scale margin per client workspace, not per seat
