**Correction to our own doctrine:** `kiwuuu-analyze` step 3 says *"mid-video 'comment X'
often outperforms end."* Kallaway's corpus says the opposite at n=51 — he places it last,
after the payload is fully delivered. Treat mid-roll CTA as unproven for teaching content.

## Re-hook phrases, counted across all 53 transcripts

| Count | Phrase pattern | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 13× | "this is where…" | Points at the next peak |
| 8× | "most people don't know this, but…" | Insider status |
| 6× | "but here's the thing / but this is where…" | Direction reversal |
| 4× | "and this is big / this is huge" | Stakes escalation |
| 2× | "1% of people know how to do this" | Scarcity of the knowledge |
| 1× each | "where most people mess up", "the money is made", "nobody talks about" | Same family |

The pattern: a re-hook is **not** a new question — it is a **status or stakes escalation
placed immediately before the next beat**. He tells you the next 10 seconds are the valuable
part, then delivers it.

## The series system (visible in the captions)

He runs numbered, named series and says the series name aloud in line one:

- **"Best Visual Hooks"** — ep 1 (Pattern Interrupts), ep 2 (Clip Stitch)…
- **"Best Viral Formats"** — ep 1 (S-Tier Ranking), ep 3 (Yapping), ep 5 (3 Levels)…
- **"How To Make Better Videos"** — ep 1, 3, 5, 6…

The episode number *is* a retention device: it implies a back catalogue and gives the
algorithm topic+format consistency, which is the thing he says it rewards above all else.
