# Pre-flight — the gate before anything ships

Run top to bottom. **A failed gate means stop and fix, not "note it and post anyway."**
Each item names where the fix lives.

---

## GATE 0 — Idea (before a word is written)

- [ ] **Is it uncommon, or common-with-a-unique-lens?** Never uncommon idea + uncommon lens —
      that combination is too niche to travel. → [ideas.md §1](../references/ideas.md)
- [ ] **Is the audience large enough to move?** An uncommon idea with tiny TAM has nobody to
      share it.
- [ ] **Is the angle shocking (entertainment) or useful (education)?** There is no third option.
- [ ] **Is it inside our one topic?** Wider means a *subtopic*, never a new topic.
- [ ] **The share test:** *would our target viewer look smart, helpful, or in-the-know to their
      peers if they shared this?* If no, it will not travel regardless of craft.
- [ ] **The commoditisation test:** could 50 other people make this? If yes, kill it or find the
      one-of-one angle only we have (a real deployment, a real number, a real failure).

## GATE 1 — Packaging (before the script)

- [ ] Title/cover written **first**. → [packaging.md](../references/packaging.md)
- [ ] The desire loop is named: which pain, which solution, which outcome.
- [ ] Text describes the **feeling or action**, not the content. Not
      "scriptwriting masterclass" — "basically cheating."
- [ ] Max **three** visual elements. Nothing in the bottom-right corner.
- [ ] Legible at 1/16th of a phone screen.

**Then, by format — the two surfaces are different things:**

- **YouTube** — title + thumbnail are consumed *together*, so the thumbnail must **not repeat
  the title**; it's a second surface that confirms the promise.
- **Short form (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)** — there is no title beside the video. The two surfaces are:
  - [ ] **In-video title text** — does the scroll-stopping. 3–5 words, big, on screen in second
        one, and *not spoken aloud*. This is what the "don't repeat" rule applies to: title text
        must not duplicate your spoken first line.
  - [ ] **Grid cover** — browsed *after* the fact, on your profile. Its job is bingeability:
        clear text saying what that video is, so a visitor scanning the grid sees which others
        they want. → [packaging.md §6](../references/packaging.md)

## GATE 2 — Outline 🚧 THE HARD GATE

- [ ] Body points bulleted, each with **what / why / how**.
- [ ] **Is at least one point genuinely non-obvious?**
      → **If no: do not write the script.** Go research more. This gate is the one most likely
      to be skipped and the one that decides whether the video was worth making.
- [ ] **Point count matches runtime** (30s→2 · 60s→3 · 90s→3–4 · 2min+→4+), and they're ordered
      **2-1-3-4 truncated to that count** — second-best first, best second. Never pad to four.
- [ ] **Native embed placed — or explicitly N/A.** Name which body point sets up our resource as
      the solve. **N/A is a valid answer in two cases:** a short (≤90s) where the CTA *is* the
      embed, and any video with no resource to embed. What fails this gate is claiming an embed
      and then not writing one.

## GATE 3 — Hook (the four commandments)

- [ ] **Alignment** — visual, on-screen title text, and spoken line all point at the same
      **idea**, in different **words**. Title text that transcribes the spoken line wastes a
      surface. → SKILL.md "Reconciling the rules" §7.
- [ ] **Speed to value** — topic legible within the first 1–2 seconds. Zero fluff lines before
      it. If line 3 is the first mention of the topic, cut lines 1 and 2.
- [ ] **Clarity** — run the **isolation test**: read only the hook. Is there more than one way
      to interpret it? Rewrite until only the intended reading survives.
- [ ] **Curiosity** — read it back alone. Does it make you want the next line?
- [ ] **The pain is framed as theirs ("you"), not yours ("I").** First person is fine — and
      often required — as *proof* ("I tested this on 287 businesses"), never as the pain
      statement ("I've struggled with…").
- [ ] Direct contrast, not implied — state the common belief, *then* flip it. Doubles the swing.
- [ ] There is a **real payoff** at the end of the promise. Running the formula with nothing at
      the end of the rainbow is how you become a fraud.
- [ ] Something **moves** on screen in second one.
- [ ] 3–5 words of **title text** on screen, big and bold.

## GATE 4 — Body & retention

- [ ] Opens at ~70/100 intensity, spikes to ~90 within 5–10 seconds.
- [ ] **A re-hook before every new point.** Roughly one peak every 10 seconds; ~3 story loops
      per 60 seconds. → [story.md §2](../references/story.md)
- [ ] Every loop closes **as** the next one opens — no gap where the viewer thinks "I'm done."
- [ ] **Hedge sweep:** search the script for *if · maybe · might · could · probably* and delete
      them. Swap **if → when**.
- [ ] The core framework has a **name**, said **immediately after the snapback closes** and
      before the first body point — sentence 2 with a one-line hook, sentence 4 after the full
      three-step hook.
- [ ] Sixth-grade reading level, active voice.
- [ ] Wide-to-narrow logic: broad context, then funnel to the point.
- [ ] There's a villain (needn't be human) and something at stake.
- [ ] **Atomic shareability:** could a viewer retell this to a friend after one watch?

## GATE 5 — Close

- [ ] Last loop **closed**. Bait fulfilled — no bait-and-switch.
- [ ] Outro leaves them on a high note and restates the pain we solved.
- [ ] Consider the loop-back line — teach them to rewatch the opening.
- [ ] **CTA — pick the right one for the account, they are not interchangeable:**

  | Account type | CTA | Success metric |
  |---|---|---|
  | Media-game / audience-building (Absurdity, REKT, BLACK BOX, THE MACHINE) | **Keyword CTA** — one asset with a number in it + one comment keyword, placed last | comments, saves, shares |
  | **Offer-led — anything selling Kiwuuu** | **Offer-led CTA** — disqualifier + the one number that identifies a real buyer + ask for that number or point at the deepest asset. → [ops.md §8b](../references/ops.md) | **qualified replies** (a reply containing a real number about their business) |

- [ ] ⚠️ **If this is a Kiwuuu video and you wrote a keyword CTA, that's a FAIL.** It harvests
      leads into a close that currently converts at zero. Five qualified replies beats 300
      comments.

## GATE 6 — Read-back (from the text alone)

1. Is this story interesting?
2. Is it as compressed as it can possibly be?
3. Does this hook actually hook me on its own?
4. What emotion do I feel when I finish reading?

- [ ] **Word count — count it, don't estimate it.** Spoken words only, excluding stage
      directions and on-screen text. Target ≈ **2.9 words/second**: 30s→~85 · 60s→~175 ·
      90s→~260 · then ~175/min. More than ~10% over budget means the read will run long or feel
      rushed — cut, don't speed up.
- [ ] **Verify the framework name's position by counting sentences**, not by intending it.
      Writing the interjection as three short sentences silently pushes the name from four to
      six. → SKILL.md "Reconciling the rules" §1.

> If it passes on text alone, ship it. Visuals only make it better.
> A verified counter for both checks is at the bottom of
> [worked-example.md](worked-example.md).

# Post-mortem — diagnosing a video that flopped

Work in this order. Stop at the first failing layer; everything below it is untestable until
that one is fixed.

**1. Did it get shown at all?** (~200 views and dead = the first sample pool rejected it.)
→ The hook or the packaging failed, or the topic didn't match the audience the algorithm has
learned to send you. → [ops.md §2](../references/ops.md)

**2. Did they leave in the first 3 seconds?** Below 70% retention at 3s on Shorts is a problem;
below 50% is a huge one. Diagnose the hook against exactly four causes, in order:

| Cause | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Delay** | Topic arrives after line 1 | Cut the preamble, promote the topic line |
| **Confusion** | Ambiguous phrasing, jargon | Simpler words, active voice, isolation test |
| **Irrelevance** | Clear but "not for me" | Frame the *pain* as "you" not "I" (proof can stay first-person); agitate a pain they already have |
| **Disinterest** | Clear and relevant, still flat | No contrast — add the common belief and flip it |

**3. Did they leave mid-video?** Watch it **one sentence at a time** and ask after each: *am I
clear what this is and where it's going?* The first sentence where the answer is no is your
drop-off. Usually one of: a loop closed with no new loop opened (missing re-hook), a
comprehension break, or the body led with its best point and value visibly declined.

**4. Did they watch but not share?** The content was clear but not *shareworthy*. It failed
novelty, on-target value, concision, or contrast. Nobody earns social credit passing it on.

**5. Did they share but not convert?** Not a content problem. Either the audience is too wide
for the offer (the **Views Trap**), or the trust is accruing to the wrong domain — they trust
you as an entertainer, not as the person who solves their specific problem.
→ [ideas.md §7](../references/ideas.md)

> ⚠️ For Kiwuuu, level 5 is where our actual constraint sits: **287 leads, 113 replies, 0
> conversions.** More views and more leads do not move that number. Before optimising any of
> gates 0–4 for reach, check whether reach is the binding constraint. It usually isn't.
