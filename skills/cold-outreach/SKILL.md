# Cold Outreach Skill

Founder-led B2B outreach system. Built for selling Kiwuuu (WhatsApp AI agents) to small and medium businesses.

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) for Kiwuuu

### Tier 1 (Highest conversion probability)
- **SMB owners with WhatsApp as primary customer channel** (retail, services, real estate)
- Business size: 2-50 employees
- WhatsApp conversations: 20+ per day
- Pain: Manual replies, missed messages, no 24/7 coverage

### Tier 2 (Good fit)
- Legal/accounting firms (high-value per client, complex questions)
- E-commerce stores (order tracking, returns via WA)
- Healthcare/dental clinics (appointment booking)

### Tier 3 (Possible but harder)
- Enterprise (long sales cycles, procurement)
- Pure online businesses (email-first, not WhatsApp-native)

## Cold Email Framework (SPEAR)

**S**ituation — acknowledge their world  
**P**roblem — name the specific pain  
**E**vidence — social proof or credibility  
**A**ction — clear, low-friction ask  
**R**esponse — make saying yes easy  

### Template A: Direct Problem-Solution
```
Subject: {company} WhatsApp → auto-replies

Hi {first_name},

Noticed {company} is active on WhatsApp — most businesses in {industry} 
are spending 2-4 hours/day on manual replies.

We built Kiwuuu: 22 AI agents that handle WhatsApp customer questions 
automatically. Customer service, sales, legal — agents that actually 
understand the question and give complete answers, 24/7.

Takes 30 minutes to set up. 100 free messages to test.

Worth a quick look? → app.kiwuuu.com

{name}
```

### Template B: Specific Pain Point
```
Subject: Missed WhatsApp messages after hours?

Hi {first_name},

If a {industry} client messages at 10pm asking about {specific_topic} — 
what happens?

Most businesses either miss it or send a generic "we'll reply tomorrow."
By morning, they've called someone else.

Kiwuuu puts an AI agent on your WhatsApp that handles these instantly — 
at 2am, weekends, whenever.

Free trial (no card): app.kiwuuu.com/signup

{name}
```

### Template C: Social Proof (once you have customers)
```
Subject: How {similar_company} automated their WhatsApp

Hi {first_name},

{similar_company} (similar to {their_company} — {industry}) was handling 
50+ WhatsApp customer queries per day manually.

After deploying Kiwuuu's AI agents:
→ 70% of queries handled automatically
→ Response time: 3 minutes average (from 4 hours)
→ 0 missed messages after hours

Worth seeing if it fits {their_company}? 

Quick demo: [link] or just reply here

{name}
```

## LinkedIn Outreach Templates

### Connection Request Note (300 chars max)
```
Hi {first_name} — I help {industry} businesses automate their WhatsApp 
customer support with AI agents. Saw {company} is growing fast. Would 
love to connect and share what we're seeing in the space.
```

### Follow-up DM after connect
```
Thanks for connecting, {first_name}!

Quick question: how are you currently handling WhatsApp customer queries 
at {company}? Curious if it's a manual process or you have something in 
place.

We just launched Kiwuuu — AI agents on WhatsApp. Happy to share what 
we're building if relevant.
```

## WhatsApp Outreach (only for warm leads)

Never cold-WA prospects. Only use WhatsApp after:
1. Email reply or LinkedIn connect
2. Or inbound request

### WhatsApp follow-up (post-email, no reply)
```
Hi {first_name}, sent you an email about WhatsApp AI agents for 
{company} — thought I'd reach out here directly too. Happy to show 
you how it works in a quick demo if you're curious. - {your_name}
```

## Sequence Cadence

### 5-touch sequence (10 days)
```
Day 1:  Email 1 (direct problem-solution)
Day 3:  LinkedIn connection request (no pitch)
Day 5:  Email 2 (different angle — social proof or specifics)
Day 7:  LinkedIn DM (if connected) — casual follow-up
Day 10: Email 3 (breakup email — "last one, no worries")
Day 10+: Move to nurture list (monthly content email)
```

### Breakup Email (always send this — highest reply rate)
```
Subject: Closing the loop

Hi {first_name},

I've reached out a couple of times about automating {company}'s WhatsApp — 
no worries if the timing's off or it's not a fit.

If you ever want to explore what 22 AI agents on WhatsApp could do for 
your team, the free trial's at app.kiwuuu.com.

Wishing you the best regardless.

{name}
```

## Personalization Variables to Research
Before outreach, find 1-2 of:
- Recent company news / product launch
- LinkedIn post they made
- Industry challenge they'd recognize
- Specific WhatsApp use case for their vertical

Rule: **1 minute of research → 10x reply rate improvement**

## Reply Handling

### "Not interested"
```
Totally understood — thanks for replying. If things change or you 
hear of someone who could use it, I'd appreciate the intro. Good luck!
```

### "Send me more info"
```
Of course! Two options:
1. Quick 10-min demo → [calendar link]
2. Start free trial yourself → app.kiwuuu.com (100 msgs, no card)

What works best?
```

### "We already use X"
```
Interesting — are you happy with it? I ask because most tools we see 
are generic chatbots. Kiwuuu's 25 agents are specialized (legal, finance, 
sales, CS) — people often run both for different use cases.

Worth seeing the difference?
```

## Tracking Metrics
- Open rate target: >40% (good subject line)
- Reply rate target: >5% (good personalization)
- Demo rate target: >30% of replies
- Trial-to-demo: >50%

Tools: Lemlist, Apollo, or just Gmail + Streak CRM
